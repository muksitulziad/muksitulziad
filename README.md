### About Me

I am a **Robotics Software & Systems Engineer** specializing in end-to-end autonomy, perception, control, and resilient communication architectures for ground rovers and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

As **Software Team Lead** for the Mars Rover Team at **AIUB Robotics Crew**, I architect and integrate real-time rover control stacks, long-range wireless telemetry, and low-latency remote operation interfaces. Beyond planetary rovers, my engineering work spans autonomous UAVs, decentralized swarm coordination, inter-drone networking, and counter-UAS interception systems—focused on **reliable autonomy, multi-robot coordination, and real-time performance under harsh real-world constraints**.
