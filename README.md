### 🤖 About Me

- 🔭 I lead software integration for our **University Rover Challenge** team, working on ROS 2, rover-to-base communication, and the operator dashboard.
- 🚁 I’m developing **multi-drone simulations** with Gazebo and ArduPilot to study coordination under delayed or unreliable communication.
- 🧪 I’m interested in **robot reliability in the field**, especially how systems respond to interrupted links and uncertain position estimates.
- 📚 I’m organizing **`robotics-research-library`** for C++ and Python implementations, simulation setups, and reproducible experiments.
- 🌱 I’m deepening my skills in **robot localization, sensor fusion, and autonomy evaluation**.
- 👯 I’m open to collaborating on **ground rover autonomy, aerial robotics, and open-source simulation**.
