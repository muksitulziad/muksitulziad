### About Me

I develop software for ground rovers and autonomous drones, with a focus on navigation, perception, and reliable communication. As software team lead for our University Rover Challenge team, I lead integration across the rover’s ROS 2 stack, wireless link, and base-station dashboard.

I’m developing multi-drone experiments in Gazebo and ArduPilot to study how communication delays and packet loss affect coordination. I’m also building a robotics research library of C++ and Python implementations, simulation setups, and reproducible experiments.

### 🧭 Robotics & Research Skills

- **Autonomous Navigation** · LiDAR and visual SLAM, AMCL, occupancy grids, pose graphs, A*, D* Lite, Hybrid A*, RRT*, PRM, DWA, MPPI, terrain-aware planning, and multi-robot exploration.
- **Control & Robotics Mathematics** · State-space modeling, PID, LQR/LQG, MPC, system identification, Lyapunov stability, trajectory optimization, SO(3)/SE(3), rigid-body dynamics, forward and inverse kinematics, and Jacobians.
- **Sensor Fusion & Estimation** · Kalman filters, EKF, UKF, particle filters, error-state EKF, IMU preintegration, factor graphs, and fusion of camera, LiDAR, IMU, GNSS, and encoder data.
- **Computer Vision & AI** · Camera calibration, stereo depth, visual odometry, YOLO, segmentation, ByteTrack/DeepSORT, ONNX, TensorRT, PyTorch, CNNs, Transformers, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning.
- **Research & Experimentation** · Literature review, baseline reproduction, ablation studies, repeated trials, statistical evaluation, reproducible benchmarks, LaTeX, and Zotero.
