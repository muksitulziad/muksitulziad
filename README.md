### 🤖 About Me

- 🛰️ I lead software integration for our **University Rover Challenge** team, connecting the rover’s ROS 2 software, motor controller, and base-station dashboard.
- 🚁 I’m developing **multi-drone simulations** with **Gazebo and ArduPilot** to study how autonomous robots coordinate under limited bandwidth and intermittent communication.
- 🧭 My research interests include **SLAM, sensor fusion, motion planning, optimal control, and multi-robot autonomy**.
- 📚 I’m organizing **`robotics-research-library`** to share C++ and Python implementations, simulation setups, and reproducible experiments.
- 🤝 I’m open to collaborating on **ground rover autonomy, aerial robotics, and open-source robotics tools**.
