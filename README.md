### About Me

I am a **Robotics Software & Systems Engineer** specializing in end-to-end autonomy, perception, control, and resilient communication architectures for ground rovers and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

As **Software Team Lead** for the Mars Rover Team at **AIUB Robotics Crew**, I architect and integrate real-time rover control stacks, long-range wireless telemetry, and low-latency remote operation interfaces. Beyond planetary rovers, my engineering work spans autonomous UAVs, decentralized swarm coordination, inter-drone networking, and counter-UAS interception systems.

- **Core Focus:** Reliable field autonomy, multi-robot coordination, and real-time system performance under harsh real-world constraints.
- **Primary Stack:** `ROS 2` · `Gazebo` · `ArduPilot` · `Computer Vision` · `Embedded Systems` · `Wireless Telemetry`

### 🧭 Robotics & Research Domains

* **Autonomous Navigation & Planning**  
  LiDAR & visual SLAM, AMCL, occupancy grids, pose graphs, A*, D* Lite, Hybrid A*, RRT*, PRM, DWA, MPPI, terrain-aware path planning, and multi-robot exploration.

* **Control Theory & Robotics Mathematics**  
  State-space modeling, PID, LQR/LQG, MPC, system identification, Lyapunov stability, trajectory optimization, $\text{SO}(3)/\text{SE}(3)$ Lie groups, rigid-body dynamics, forward/inverse kinematics, and Jacobians.

* **Sensor Fusion & State Estimation**  
  Kalman Filters (KF, EKF, UKF), Error-State EKF (ES-EKF), particle filters, IMU preintegration, factor graphs, and multi-sensor fusion (camera, LiDAR, IMU, GNSS, wheel encoders).

* **Computer Vision & Embodied AI**  
  Camera calibration, stereo depth estimation, visual odometry, object detection (YOLO), semantic segmentation, multi-object tracking (ByteTrack, DeepSORT), ONNX, TensorRT, PyTorch, CNNs, Transformers, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning.

* **Research Methodology & Benchmarking**  
  Literature review, baseline reproduction, ablation studies, repeated field trials, statistical evaluation, reproducible simulation benchmarks, $\LaTeX$, and Zotero.
